I'm Bismarck Nana Konadu, the Co-Founder and Lead Developer at Sonnami Studios. My journey in game development started with a fascination for interactive storytelling and systems design.

At Sonnami, we create atmospheric games like Neon Drift and Echoes of the Void, blending tight mechanics with immersive environments. Beyond games, I build open-source tools like the Sonnami YT Downloader to solve real-world problems.
