About Me

Building Worlds &
Apps That Matter

I'm Bismarck Nana Konadu, the Co-Founder and Lead Developer at Sonnami Studios. A little while back, I was honoured to receive the Mid Tech Code Society Best Student Award 2024 — a lovely nod to the work I've been pouring into software development.

At Sonnami, we create atmospheric games like Neon Drift and Echoes of the Void, blending tight mechanics with immersive environments. My latest app, Weather Hub, brings accurate, minimal weather forecasting to Ghana and beyond.

I believe in clean code, thoughtful design, and pushing the boundaries of what African developers can build. When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring new game mechanics or contributing to open-source projects.
